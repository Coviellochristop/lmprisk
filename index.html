Why a captive

The case for insuring your own risk.

A captive is an insurance company formed to underwrite the risks of its owner. For the right organization, it turns insurance from a sunk cost into a strategic asset.

01

Retain underwriting profit

Keep the margin that would otherwise stay with a commercial carrier when losses come in below premium.

02

Capture investment income

Reserves held in the captive can earn investment income for the benefit of the owner.

03

Reduce market reliance

Gain stability and independence from the pricing cycles of the commercial insurance market.

04

Tailor your coverage

Design coverage to your actual exposures, including risks the commercial market prices poorly or won't write.

Regulatory & domicile expertise

Licensed where it counts, fluent in compliance.

Choosing a domicile and staying in good standing are where many captives stumble. LMP is a licensed captive manager in both the United States and the Turks & Caicos Islands, and we keep your captive aligned with its regulator year-round.

Domicile selection

The right home for your captive

Domicile shapes capital requirements, taxation, regulatory burden, and cost. We help you weigh US onshore options against an offshore structure, then stand the captive up where it best fits your goals.

  • US and offshore (TCI) domicile evaluation
  • Capitalization and regulatory-requirement analysis
  • Business-plan development and regulator submission
  • Incorporation and licensing coordination
Ongoing compliance

In good standing, year after year

Once licensed, the work shifts to disciplined regulatory and financial administration. We maintain the records, filings, and governance your domicile requires and liaise with regulators on your behalf.

  • Regulatory filings and statutory reporting
  • Liaison with domicile regulators
  • Governance, board resolutions, and corporate records
  • Healthcare-regulatory experience, including Medicaid requirements
